\chapter{Project Evidence and Completeness Review}\label{ch:project-evidence-and-completeness-review}

The inventory below is the evidence boundary used for this first draft.
`Confirmed' means directly supported by accessible repository content or
an official project record; it does not imply system-level validation.
Confidence reflects the quality and specificity of the evidence, not
confidence that a subsystem meets an unstated performance target.
