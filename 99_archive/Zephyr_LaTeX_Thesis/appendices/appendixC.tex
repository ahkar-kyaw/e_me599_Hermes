\chapter{Pin, Wiring, and Connector Tables}\label{ch:pin-wiring-and-connector-tables}

The pin maps below reproduce the active ESP32 prototype evidence
available during drafting. They are not a wiring authorization. Crossed
transmit\slash{}receive naming, board silkscreen, logic voltage, connector
orientation, and ground reference must be verified at the installed
hardware before energization.
