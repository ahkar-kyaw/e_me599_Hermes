\chapter{Required Figures and Tables Checklist}\label{ch:required-figures-and-tables-checklist}

All figure placeholders in the thesis include a proposed number,
caption, content description, and likely source. Table I.1 consolidates
their production route. Every completed image should be at publication
resolution, use legible labels, identify whether it is original or
adapted, and preserve editable source files.
