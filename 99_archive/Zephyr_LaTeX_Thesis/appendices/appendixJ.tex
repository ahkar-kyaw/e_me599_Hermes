\chapter{Items Needed From the Author}\label{ch:items-needed-from-the-author}

Priority 1 items block defensible modeling, safety review, or
experimental claims. Priority 2 items are needed for a complete thesis
but do not prevent continued drafting. Priority 3 items improve
presentation and archival quality.
